Common questions

How do I start over?

Open Stats and tap Reset progress. Your level, XP, chips, stats and badges go back to the start.

I ran out of chips.

The chips are free. When you run low, the table offers a refill.

How do I change my name or avatar?

Tap your avatar on the home screen.

How do I turn the sound off?

Tap the speaker button on the home screen. The app also stays quiet when your silent switch is on.

Can I win real money?

No. Blackjack Buddy is for practice and fun. The chips have no cash value, and the app has no purchases.

What rules does the table use?

6 decks, dealer stands on soft 17, double after split allowed, and blackjack pays 3 to 2. The strategy chart in the app matches these rules.

Does the app collect my data?

No. Everything stays on your iPhone. Read the privacy policy.
