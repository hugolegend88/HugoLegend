Blackjack Buddy

Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026.

Blackjack Buddy collects no personal data. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us or to anyone else.

What the app stores

The app saves the following on your iPhone so you can pick up where you left off:

  • The name you type and the avatar you pick.
  • A photo, only if you choose one from your photo library. The app gets only the photo you pick, not your whole library.
  • Your chips, level, XP, badges and practice stats.
  • Whether sound is on or off.

This information never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes all of it. You can also clear your progress any time with Stats, then Reset progress.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No network connections. The app works fully offline.

Real money

Blackjack Buddy is a practice game that uses free play chips. It offers no real-money gambling, and the chips have no cash value.

Children

Blackjack Buddy simulates a casino game and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new effective date.
