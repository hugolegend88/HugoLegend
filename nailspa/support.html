Common questions

How do I switch salons?

Tap "Switch salon" on the home screen, or tap the salon's logo, then pick the other salon.

How do I change or cancel a booking?

Open My visits and enter the mobile number you booked with. The salon texts you a one-time code, and then you can move or cancel the appointment.

Who answers the concierge chat?

An AI (Grok, by xAI) answers first, and it hands you to the salon manager when you need a person. The app asks your permission before your first message. You can always book on the Book tab or call instead.

I work at one of the salons. How do I sign in?

Pick your salon, open the Staff tab and enter your staff PIN. Ask your salon manager if you don't have one.

What happens to my information?

It goes only to the salon you choose. Read the privacy policy.
